What this is

We searched for INTVL the way a new runner in Japan or Korea does, then rebuilt what happens after the tap.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. For INTVL we looked at two things. First, your app: your App Store listings in the US, Japan and Korea, intvl.com.au, and the five ideas from our earlier INTVL rebuild. Second, the App Store searches that lead to it: three Apple Ads keywords, all the name of another territory app, running in Japan and Korea only, and none on “INTVL” anywhere.

Your Japan and Korea listings already have their own subtitle, description and screenshots. The app behind them is in English. What follows is five ideas for the flow you have, and two keyword flows that show what happens when the runner in Tokyo and the runner in Seoul each get their own version. Every one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and all of it is clickable. The prototype uses your design, not ours.
