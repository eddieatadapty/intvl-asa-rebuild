# Search to Pro · INTVL

Five ranked ideas for INTVL's onboarding and paywall, plus two versions of the flow side by side: one for people who searched "kyro" on the App Store, one for "kyro: 영토 점령 러닝 게임". Prepared by Adapty.

**→ https://eddieatadapty.github.io/intvl-asa-rebuild/**

Opens on the Overview. `?open=proto` lands on the prototype; `&step=<screen>` opens both phones on one screen.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Let people plan their first capture before the paywall | CR +10–20% to purchase |
| 02 | Ask how they want to play, then say it back | CR +8–15% to purchase |
| 03 | Sell Pro in the game’s own terms | CR +5–12% to purchase |
| 04 | Show the annual saving and the proof next to the button | ARPU +5–10% more players on annual |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | CR +4–8% to purchase |

## What differs by keyword

| Screen | "kyro" | "kyro: 영토 점령 러닝 게임" |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| App Store | Typed “kyro” · your Japanese subtitle, captions in Japanese, first tile names Tokyo | Typed the Korean listing name · your Korean subtitle, captions in Korean, first tile names Seoul |
| Welcome | “Run a loop. Claim Tokyo.” in Japanese | The same promise in Korean, with Seoul |
| First loop | The loop is labelled Shibuya, Tokyo | The loop is labelled Gangnam, Seoul |
| Payoff | “Your first block of Shibuya” | “Your first block of Gangnam” |
| Paywall | “Rule Shibuya” · ¥7,000 a year, ¥583 a month | “Rule Gangnam” · ₩70,000 a year, ₩5,833 a month |
| Offer on close | First year for ¥4,400, or keep playing free (hypothesis) | First year for ₩44,000, or keep playing free (hypothesis) |

Every price, rating and feature comes from INTVL's App Store listing, website or in-app screens; Apple Ads figures from Adapty's research. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for INTVL.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
